## arXiv re-verification (the rate-limit repair)

Every one of the 38 ids was re-read once, serially, from
`https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=…` in batches of five with a 4 s gap, reading
`<title>`, first `<author>`, `<published>` and the version suffix. All 38 resolved; no id
needed correcting, and the returned titles match the research files. The API metadata is also
where the `[authors year]` citation in the index comes from.

Citation rule used for the inserted rows: the year is the venue year where the research note
fixed one (the ten probabilistic rows), otherwise the arXiv first-publication year. Author
form follows the index: `surname et al. year`, `surname and surname year` for two authors,
bare `surname year` for one, lowercased.

## Non-arXiv rows

- **LightGBM** — NeurIPS 2017 PDF at
  `proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/file/6449f44a102fde848669bdd9eb6b76fa-Paper.pdf`
  (HTTP 200, `application/pdf`, 366 KB), fetched through the existing `neurips` route family.
- **Meinshausen, Quantile Regression Forests** — JMLR 7 PDF at
  `jmlr.org/papers/volume7/meinshausen06a/meinshausen06a.pdf` (200, `application/pdf`,
  800 KB), fetched through `direct-pdf`.
- **Bornhuetter & Ferguson 1972** — the research file gives it a free CAS proceedings scan
  (`casact.org/sites/default/files/database/proceed_proceed72_72181.pdf`, 200,
  `application/pdf`, 786 KB), not a DOI: PCAS has no DOI at all. It therefore enters as
  `[pdf]` and is fetched, not citation-only. This is one row different from the approval's
  "8 DOI-only" wording, which counted it among the eight non-arXiv actuarial rows; inventing
  a DOI would be worse than fetching the scan, and `PROVENANCE.md` puts reachable non-arXiv
  payload in scope. Recorded here and in the PR body.
- **Seven DOI-only actuarial rows** — citation-only, `ROUTES` specs with
  `absence="paywall"` where the publisher withholds the full text (Mack/ASTIN,
  England–Verrall/BAJ, Frees/Shi/Valdez/ASTIN, Jørgensen/T&F, Lambert/JSTOR,
  Mullahy/Elsevier) and `absence="unknown"` for Blier-Wong et al. 2021 (MDPI, open access
  but 403 to a plain client — bot defence, no observable paywall). No payload, no licence
  record, as approved.
